Between the numbers 600 and 700, which numbers have the digital root: (i) 5, (ii) 7, (iii) 3?

(i) Digital root 5, from 600 to 700 are (Sum of digits are 5(1 + 4 or 2 + 3)) 608, 617, 626, 635, 644, 653, 662, 671, 680, 689, 698.

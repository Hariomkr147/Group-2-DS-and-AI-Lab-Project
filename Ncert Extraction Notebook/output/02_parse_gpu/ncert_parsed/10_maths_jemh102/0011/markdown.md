Example 2 : Find the zeroes of the quadratic polynomial $x ^ { 2 } + 7 x + 1 0$ , and verify the relationship between the zeroes and the coefficients.

Solution : We have

$$
x^{2}+7x+10=(x+2)(x+5)
$$

$\mathrm { S o } ,$ the value of $x ^ { 2 } + 7 x + 1 0$ is zero when $x + 2 = 0   or   x + 5 = 0.$ , i.e., when $x = - 2$ or $x = - 5$ . Therefore, the zeroes of $x^{2}+7x+10\mathrm{are}=2$ and – 5. Now,

$$
sum of zeros  = -2 + (-5) = -7 = \frac{-(7)}{1} = \frac{( Coefficient of   x)}{ Coefficient of   x^2}
$$

$$
product of zeros  = (-2) \times (-5) = 10 = \frac{10}{1} = \frac{ constant term }{ coefficient of   x^2}.
$$

So, the value of $p(x) = 2x^2 - 8x + 6$ is zero when $x - 1 = 0   or   x - 3 = 0.$ , i.e., when $x = 1   or   x = 3.   So$ , the zeroes of $2 x ^ { 2 } - 8 x + 6$ are 1 and 3. Observe that :

Product of its zeroes $s = 1 \times 3 = 3 = \frac{6}{2} = \frac{ constant term }{ coefficient of   x^{2}}$

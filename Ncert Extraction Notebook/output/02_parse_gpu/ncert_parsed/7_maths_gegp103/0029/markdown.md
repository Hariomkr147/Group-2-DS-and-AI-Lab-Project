## 3.7 Addition and Subtraction of Decimals

？ Priya requires 2.7 m of cloth for her skirt, and Shylaja requires 3.5m for her kurti. What is the total quantity of cloth needed?

We have to find the sum of $2.7 m  + 3.5 m$

Earlier, we saw how to add $2   \frac { 7 } { 1 0 } \pm   3   \frac { 5 } { 1 0 }$ (also shown below). Can you carry out the same addition using decimal notation? It is shown below. Share your observations.

The total quantity of cloth needed is 6.2 m.

$$
\begin{aligned}&\frac{\begin{array}{r}2 \quad \frac{7}{10} \\+ 3 \quad \frac{5}{10} \\\end{array}}{\begin{array}{r}= \quad 5 \quad \frac{12}{10} \\\end{array}} \\= \quad 6 \quad \frac{2}{10}\end{aligned}
$$

How much longer is Shylaja’s cloth compared to Priya’s?

We have to find the difference of 3.5m – 2.7m. Again, observe how the differences $3\frac{5}{10} \sim 2\frac{7}{10}$ and $3.5m = 2.7m$ are computed.

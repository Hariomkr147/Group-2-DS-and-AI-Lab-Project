In this chapter, you have studied the following points:

2. A real number   is said to be a root of the quadratic equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ , if $a \alpha ^ { 2 } + b \alpha + c = 0$ . The zeroes of the quadratic polynomial $a x ^ { 2 } + b x + c$ and the roots of the quadratic equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ are the same.

**3.** If we can factorise $ax^{2}+bx+c,a \ne 0$ , into a product of two linear factors, then the roots of the quadratic equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ can be found by equating each factor to zero.

**4.** Quadratic formula: The roots of a quadratic equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ are given by $\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^{2}-4ac}}{2a}$ , provided $b ^ { 2 }   -   4 a c   \geq   0$

**5.** A quadratic equation $ax^{2}+bx+c=0$ has

(i) two distinct real roots, if $b^{2}-4ac>0$

(ii) two equal roots (i.e., coincident roots), if $b^{2}-4ac=0$ , and

(iii) no real roots, if $b^{2}-4ac<0$

**Solution :** The prime factorisation of 96 and 404 gives :

$$
9 6 = 2 ^ { 5 } \times 3 , 4 0 4 = 2 ^ { 2 } \times 1 0 1
$$

Therefore, the HCF of these two integers is $2 ^ { 2 } = 4$

Also,

$$
\mathrm{LCM}(96,404)=\frac{96 \times 404}{\mathrm{HCF}(96,404)}=\frac{96 \times 404}{4}=9696
$$

**Example 4 :** Find the HCF and LCM of 6, 72 and 120, using the prime factorisation method.

**Solution :** We have :

$$
6 = 2 \times 3, \; 72 = 2^3 \times 3^2, \; 120 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 5
$$

Here, $2 ^ { 1 }$ and $3 ^ { 1 }$ are the smallest powers of the common factors 2 and 3, respectively. So, HCF (6, 72, 120) = 2<sup>1</sup> × 3<sup>1</sup> = 2 × 3 = 6

2<sup>3</sup>, $3 ^ { 2 }$ and $5 ^ { 1 }$ are the greatest powers of the prime factors 2, 3 and 5 respectively involved in the three numbers.

So, LCM (6, 72, 120) = 2<sup>3</sup> × 3<sup>2</sup> × 5<sup>1</sup> = 360

**Remark :** Notice, $6 \times 72 \times 120 \neq  HCF$ (6, 72, 120) × LCM (6, 72, 120). So, the product of three numbers is not equal to the product of their HCF and LCM.

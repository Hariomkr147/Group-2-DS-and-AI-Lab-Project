The concept of weighted arithmetic mean was also used by Indian mathematicians like Śrīdharācārya (c. 750 CE) to estimate the purity of gold-alloys (essentially today's karat calculations).

The concept of the weighted mean eventually appeared in other parts of the world, e.g., Europe, where it gained prominence by the 1700s. The weighted mean now plays a crucial role across mathematics and statistics, allowing us to combine data of different sizes or importance into one number to give an overall accurate picture.

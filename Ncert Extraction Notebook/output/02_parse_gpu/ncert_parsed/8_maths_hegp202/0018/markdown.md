What is the sum of the first (n – 1) odd numbers? Thus,

$$
(n - 1)^{2} + (2n - 1) = n^{2}.
$$

Note that this equation could also have been directly obtained by expanding $( n - 1 ) ^ { 2 }$ and adding $2 n - 1$ to it.

If the nth odd number, $2 n - 1$ , is also a square number, then we have a sum of two square numbers equal to another square number. We will use this idea to generate Baudhāyana triples.

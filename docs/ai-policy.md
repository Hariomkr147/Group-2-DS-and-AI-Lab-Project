# Policy on Fair and Responsible use of AI & LLMs

AI tools may supplement learning and accelerate development, but must never substitute for the team's understanding, critical thinking, experimentation or ownership of the project.

Source: https://bsc-iitm.github.io/dsai-lab-project/ai_policy.html

## Fair Use of AI & LLMs

- **Permitted use:** Teams may use LLMs to supplement their learning, clarify concepts, explore alternative approaches, generate ideas, and assist with debugging.
- **Ownership:** The core implementation, experimentation, evaluation, and conclusions must reflect the team's own understanding and effort.
- **Validation:** All AI-generated code or content must be thoroughly reviewed, validated and adapted.
- **Assessment:** While the assessment will evaluate the quality of the deliverable, it will also check the team's demonstrated understanding and individual competence. Team members must be prepared to independently explain, justify, debug and modify their submitted work.

## Restrictions & use of AI Agents in GitHub Project Repositories

- **Prohibited:** Teams must not use autonomous AI agents or AI-powered tools to perform direct project operations in GitHub. This includes:
  - Creating, modifying, or deleting files
  - Committing or pushing code
  - Creating, reviewing, approving, or merging pull requests
  - Raising or closing issues
  - Modifying project boards, workflows, or repository settings
  - Generating or updating project reports and documentation directly within the repository.
- **Manual Execution:** While AI may advise on Git commands, diagnose errors or recommend workflows, team members must understand, evaluate, and manually execute all GitHub operations themselves.
- **Permitted:** Routine, predefined automation, such as GitHub Actions running continuous integration (CI/CD) tests or linters, are permitted.

### 2. Significant Prompts and Responses

Document significant AI interactions that influenced the project's design, implementation, experiments, analysis, or conclusions. For each interaction, provide:

- **Tool Name and Problem Addressed:**
- **Prompt(s):** The key prompts used.
- **Relevant Response(s):** The portions that materially contributed to the work.
- **Action Taken:** How the team evaluated, adapted, tested, or rejected the suggestions.

*(Note: Provide a shareable conversation link wherever available. Otherwise, include the relevant prompt-response excerpts. Complete conversation logs are not required unless specifically requested.)*

### 3. Team Accountability Declaration

The disclosure must include a confirmation that:

- All AI-generated code, content, and recommendations incorporated into the project have been reviewed and appropriately validated by the team.
- Project decisions, experimental results, and conclusions are supported by the team's independent reasoning and evidence.
- Every team member understands their contributions and can explain and defend the relevant work independently.
